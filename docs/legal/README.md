# Legal Workspace

This folder contains working documents for GDPR/AVG and legal compliance, framed primarily for **Belgium**: EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR) and Belgian implementing law (e.g. Act of 30 July 2018 on the protection of natural persons regarding processing of personal data). **Not legal advice** — sign‑off with Belgian counsel where needed.

## Scope

- Define roles and responsibilities (tenant vs WinProx)
- Define lawful basis for each processing activity
- Define data retention and deletion windows
- Define data subject rights handling flow
- Track status and ownership per legal task

## Files

- `gdpr-checklist.md` - implementation checklist with owners and status
- `roles-and-responsibilities.md` - controller/processor split by feature
- `data-retention-policy.md` - retention periods per data category
- `data-subject-requests.md` - operational outline for rights requests (not legal advice)
- `release-compliance-checklist.md` - quick release checks when changing legal/privacy/retention behaviour
- `processing-register-draft.md` - draft table for processing activities (ROPA-style), to validate internally

## How to use

1. Complete all `TODO` items with legal counsel.
2. Keep decisions dated and signed off internally.
3. Update product and privacy texts after each approved legal change.
